APM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

APM (Assistant Property Manager) is a property management app and web dashboard for landlords, available as a mobile app and at portal.assistantpm.ai. APM is operated by Jose Rodriguez ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information APM handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and sign-in details.
  • Property information you enter or upload: properties, leases, tenants, vendors, work orders, insurance records, payments, and documents and photos.
  • Communications: calls, text messages, and messages sent through APM, including phone numbers, content, and timestamps.
  • Technical information: device and app details, logs, and crash reports used to keep APM working.

Information about tenants and other people

Landlords may enter information about their tenants, vendors, and other contacts, and those people may communicate through APM. We handle that information to provide the service to the landlord. Tenants should contact their landlord first with questions about their tenancy.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google," APM asks Google only for your basic profile (the openid, email, and profile permissions). We use your name and email address to create or find your APM account and sign you in, and we keep your name, email address, and a record that your account uses Google sign-in.

APM does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or other Google account data.

We use information received from Google only to provide sign-in and your APM account. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it except with service providers that help us run APM or when required by law. APM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and support APM.
  • To carry out actions you request, such as sending messages, placing calls, and preparing documents.
  • To fix problems and improve APM.
  • To meet legal obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run APM, such as phone and messaging, AI processing, crash reporting, email, and data storage providers.
  • People you choose to communicate or share with through APM.
  • Government agencies, courts, or others when required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information while your account is active and as needed afterward for legal and business records. To ask for access, correction, or deletion of your information, including your account, email g2032165@gmail.com. Certain information may be retained where the law requires it. You can also remove APM's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
